A FEW THINGS YOU MAY WONDER

Questions, gently answered.

What is AKSH? ＋

AKSH is a mental wellness ecosystem focused on personal wellbeing, learning, community and connection.

How do I book a session? ＋

Use the Book a Session link to reach out through the contact section and share what kind of support you’re looking for.

Can I use the AI companion for mental health emergencies? ＋

No. AI AKSH is not an emergency service or a substitute for professional care. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted person now.
